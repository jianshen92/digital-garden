---
title: Language as a Tool for Self Expression
date: 2024-07-25
---
One day, I realized that mastery of a language is incredibly important because it is a crucial tool for expressing and communicating with ourselves.

Much of our learning comes from internal monologue, and without the ability to express our complex and abstract emotions, we face a barrier to achieving higher self-realization.

For example, if we find it difficult to express certain feelings, we won't be able to fully understand them.
