About 3-4 months after ChatGPT was released in November 2022, I was inspired to make my own prediction about what the future world will look like with "AI" on the rise. At that point, everyone ranging from industry experts to your neighborhood aunties was making predictions about the future.

Here's mine:

1. In general, I am optimistic about the human race overall. There might be local minima—events that have set back our progress—but civilization has been trending in a positive direction over many centuries. I think this time is no different. In other words, I think the chances of Skynet enslaving humans are slim.


2. More small teams will be enabled to achieve more with less. This means we will see less dominance by tech monopolies like Grab, Uber, and Amazon, because the technical bar to create another rideshare or listing app is being lowered significantly. However, increased reliance on AI may produce a new wave of "AI monopolies" like Anthropic—which is, by the way, backed by Amazon. Things may overall be back to square one.


3. With AI-generated ~~garbage~~ content circulating in every crack of our lives, TRUST is at an all-time low. Appreciation of authentic human creations across all industries, especially artistic ones, will rise.


4. Lastly, I see the future becoming more communal and less centralized. With AI displacing many jobs that currently exist, more people will seek niche jobs and more "basic" jobs—like selling chicken rice, plumbing, carpentry—aimed at serving the local community. Combined with point number 3, I predict we will seek more genuine human interactions. Instead of being trapped in a dystopian world where all we interact with is virtual reality, I predict we will return to a more tribal, communal, physical mode of civilization like back in the day.


I often brainstorm and bounce ideas nowadays with LLMs, and I asked Claude Sonnet 4 to comment on my blog, with the following prompt.j
