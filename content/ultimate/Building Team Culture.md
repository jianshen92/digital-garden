This marks my Fourth Season into coaching a frisbee team. 

The difference is, this time we seemingly have succeeded to build a culture that we can be proud off. Whatever this culture was, it is called the -- The Tapir Standard.

Some notes on Culture Building:

1. It must be top down. Meaning: it all starts from the party with the largest say in the team.

2. Have a few leaders of the team who believe in your vision.

3. Then stick through the vision, execute no matter what. No exceptions.

4. Once it reaches a critical mass, probably 60-70% of the team, it becomes the identity of the team. The buy for the rest, will eventually happen.

5. Shaping culture is difficult. You will get pushbacks. The path can be lonely as the visionary as people are [[Aversion|averse]] to changes.

6. It may take couple of tries. But never lose sight on yourself. 

7. It takes huge emotional stability as the one who push for changes. 

8. Lastly, 心存善念，尽力而为。

I am forever grateful for the Tapir 2025 rendition on showing myself, and the world, how far a team who cares for each other can go.
